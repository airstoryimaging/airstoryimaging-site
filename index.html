About

Hi, I'm Dave.

I'm a Bay Area commercial drone pilot and licensed private pilot. Aerial imaging runs in the family: my dad ran Dixon Aerial Services, an aerial photography business, and we still own a 1929 Fairchild 71, an antique airplane that was one of the first built for aerial mapping.

Airstory Imaging carries that on with drones and digital mapping. I fly carefully, plan around airspace and weather, and deliver files you can actually use.
