About

Hi, I'm Dave.

I'm a Bay Area drone pilot and licensed private pilot. Flying runs in the family: my dad ran Dixon Aerial Services, and we still own a Fairchild Model 71, a classic aerial photography plane from the 1920s.

Airstory Imaging is how I carry that on with modern tools. I fly carefully, plan around airspace and weather, and deliver files you can actually use.
